Pricing questions

Common questions about plans and billing.

Do you offer non-profit or research discounts?

Yes. Public schools, registered non-profits, and academic researchers are eligible for discounted pricing. Contact us with proof of status.

How is usage measured for EdTech and Enterprise plans?

We plan to meter on prompts inspected, with a tier of monthly volume included and overage charged at a per-million-prompts rate. Final billing terms are confirmed in your contract during onboarding.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time, prorated to the day. Annual plans can be converted to monthly with a small administrative fee.

What's your compliance posture?

We sign Data Processing Agreements with every customer and are GDPR compliant by design. SOC 2 Type II is targeted for Q1 2027. For details on compliance, see our Enterprise page.

What's not included?

Custom model fine-tuning and bespoke integrations are scoped separately. Contact us if you have specific compliance or infrastructure requirements.
